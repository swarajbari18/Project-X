Before writing "Open" on an item in the Tend architecture files, check the construction record and the Section 10 status lines. If the owner decided the item, accepted a recommendation, or a line says "decided", "settled", "agreed", or "recorded", the item is decided. Say which record decides it, correct the label, and do not ask again.

**Why:** The owner found that an earlier pass kept labelling settled points as open and asked about them again. That wasted the owner's time and created confusion.

The same holds for corrections. On 29 Oct 2026 Claude "fixed" routing so it looked at past situations only on explicit references to earlier work. That overturned the 3 Oct agreed picture (`architecture/how_tend_finds_the_right_situation.md`: "a small set of his own finished situations that could be that purchase"), and was caught only during readability audits. Before tightening or removing any rule, search the agreed records for it.

The same holds for questions. On 1 Nov 2026, during the stack talk, Claude ended a front-end answer by asking "tree or graph?" for the journey view. The architecture already decides that: owners see a straight line by default, with a switch to the map. Swaraj said: "are asking question for the answer of which are already present in knowledge base and the architecture … Just tell me the programming languages and what frameworks should we use based on our product." For a stack or framework choice he wants a short, direct recommendation, the reasons tied to the product, and why the alternatives lose, so he can decide. Don't end with a question the record already answers.

**How to apply:** When the record has no decision, make the call yourself. Say it in plain words, mark it "call, not yet confirmed", and move on. Ask the owner only about a fact that only the owner can know. Keep decided, open, and provisional items visibly separate. Write in short sentences, and do not use codes or pointers to other files in the visible text.
