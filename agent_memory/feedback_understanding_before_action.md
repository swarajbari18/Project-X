Before routing or any other action, Tend must first understand the arrival: what its terms mean in this business (business events like "the Diwali sale", aliases, earlier readings). Routing is the result of understanding (Level 2: conversation manager's MEANING step, then VALIDATE).

**Why:** On 29 Oct 2026 Swaraj found the 28 Oct memory and knowledge design and test 1 routed Arjun's "Diwali sale" message without ever working out what the sale was (event blocked by the sender's view; meaning only reached the worker at W1). He called it careless and "highly unacceptable": understanding first would have turned routing retrieval into one simple date filter.

**How to apply:** In every design and every story walk, check that each step understands the meaning of terms before it searches, routes, plans or acts; a test that gets the right result without that understanding is a FAIL, not a PASS. Related: [[project-memory-loop-redesign]].
