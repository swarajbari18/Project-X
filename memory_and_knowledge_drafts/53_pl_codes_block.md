
## Codes added 2026-10-29, later (the product loop)

Used in this folder's files from later on 2026-10-29, when the project owner asked for loops built from the product's stages ("the loops do not look natural to me … I am first and foremost a product engineer"). Their home is the Level 2 page `knowledge_base/Level 2/the_product_loop/how_does_the_product_loop_work.md` (cited below as "the product loop"). These codes belong to Section 12 only; Sections 6 to 11 were not changed. Rows with "Call" carry a choice the design made that the project owner has not yet confirmed.

| Code | Plain meaning | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PL-1 | Tend's product loop is four loops (arrival, situation, update, message), each made of stages a capable person would recognise. Any stage may be passed when it has nothing to do, or end the loop when its finding makes the rest pointless. Fixed code chooses every pass and ending from facts the stages wrote; the AI never decides to continue. | The product loop, parts A and the one-page; owner 2026-10-29 ("Each step had a option to whether go to the next step of the loop or whether to exit right there and there"); harness study 11.2.4 | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-2 | The effort a message gets follows what it needs (whether each ask is about the business, what Tend already holds, whether the fit is settled, free text or typed, unknowns that matter, new claims on held fields), never the prompt and never a model's score. Inside a stage, turns follow the work within code's limits and the progress rule. | The product loop, A3 and G; owner 2026-10-29 ("the harness should be flexible enough that the effort auto adjusts"); harness study 10.2.3 | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-3 | Understanding the arrival is the conversation manager's one AI stage. Before anything acts on an arrival, it writes the understanding artifact (each ask and whether it is about the business; what each refers to, time words resolved; claims; unknowns; whether it answers a question Tend asked; earlier work or a past purchase; what Tend holds that answers it; where each ask fits). It uses only what Tend holds, never a business system or a person. Formerly the "reading". | The product loop, C2; owner 2026-10-29 ("understanding should be a component in the conversation manager itself"); Level 2 understanding page ("does not fetch new information") | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-4 | The wider look: when an ask needs it, memory and knowledge narrows the sender's own situations, open and finished, by the values understanding resolved (exact values first, description only inside them, with the total); an empty set means new work with no second pass; otherwise understanding chooses among the set. | The product loop, C2; owner 2026-10-29 ("filter its situation model and the retrieval filters, and then give it a better subset"); agreed 2026-10-03 | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-5 | The arrival loop's endings, chosen by fixed code from the understanding artifact: reply (no ask about the business); nothing to do; answered from what Tend holds; hold and ask; routed. An arrival may take more than one. | The product loop, C2; owner 2026-10-29 | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-6 | Routing is fixed code. It checks the understanding artifact (quotes in the arrival; situations presented; resolved values cite presented items; the ending's condition) and carries it out: attach, create, split, hold, or mark. A failed check goes back to understanding once; a second failure goes to the person named for arrivals Tend could not place. | The product loop, C3; owner 2026-10-29 ("Routing, I don't think would be much of a LLM thing"; "So routing is more code based") | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-7 | An arrival that is not about the business starts no situation; it is kept in the person's message history with its understanding artifact, marked; memory and knowledge never learns from it and never presents it to a situation. | The product loop, C2; owner 2026-10-29 ("This message will only be a noise in our knowledge framework") | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-8 | The reply decision (fixed code) decides whether and how to answer an arrival that ended at understanding: a greeting gets a reply unless a newer arrival from the person is queued or a greeting was already answered since their last business message (Call); a general question or a sum gets a short answer unless the business switched this off for customers (Call); a thank-you that answers nothing gets no reply unless the business set a courtesy reply (Call); "answered from what Tend holds" tells the items; "hold and ask" asks. It is saved with the arrival, and counts as the send check's saved decision. | The product loop, C4; owner 2026-10-29 ("you immediately answer back with hi, how are you"); Level 2 communication ("Tend should not communicate merely because it can produce words"; "Unrelated, unbounded or prohibited communication remains outside Tend's permitted behaviour"); decided 2026-10-09 (the update decision) | Decided, with three calls not yet confirmed |
| PL-9 | A reply to an arrival that is not about the business carries no claim about the business. It may repeat a question Tend already asked this person, and say that the rest of a mixed message is being worked on. The send check enforces it. | The product loop, C4 and E2 | Decided (the design's reading, from the owner's 2026-10-29 examples and Level 2 communication) |
| PL-10 | Answered from what Tend holds: every business ask is answered by an item that counts as evidence (a current claim, by the business's currentness rule, in the sender's own situation the arrival fits by a clear match, with no open conflict on its field; or a fact the business configured, read from the configuration's own store, never a cache), the sender may be told each item, and the arrival brings no new claim. The arrival is still routed, marked; the worker records the asks as answered. | The product loop, C2; owner 2026-09-05 (harness study, Part 13: "If the conversation manager feels that the situation model itself has enough information to answer the user back, it just does that"), restored 2026-10-29; harness study 14.2 (the cache never guards a decision) | Decided (the project owner, 2026-09-05; restored 2026-10-29) |
| PL-11 | Understanding the situation runs only when the version holds something not yet placed (an arrival bringing claims, new asks, a correction or a document; a free-text result; a document); it is passed for a new situation, typed results, and an arrival with nothing to place. | The product loop, D2 | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-12 | Gathering is a stage: its information plan (what could change the next safe step, which source, what order) is saved in the situation; each entry runs one bounded pass of the steps that can run now; explore steps read one source at a time within a read limit; it only reads; it never asks a person, acts, or judges whether information is enough; it is passed when memory and knowledge maps every unknown that matters to an item that counts as evidence. | The product loop, D3; Level 1 ("understanding, gathering, deciding and acting"); owner 2026-09-04 (Fork A, changed 2026-10-29) | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-13 | Weighing evidence is fixed code; it runs when new claims came in, sets evidence states, applies the business's order of sources, and marks what a conflict blocks. | The product loop, D4; Level 2 trust and evidence | Decided |
| PL-14 | Deciding is one structured answer in every round that reaches it: it checks the work against what was first asked and names the next behaviour; fixed code checks its shape, version, evidence, tools and permission. | The product loop, D5; decided 2026-10-28 (deciding is checking the work) | Decided |
| PL-15 | Acting is fixed code on one path per kind of step; the next round starts where what came back points (free text → understanding the situation; typed claims → weighing; a named need → gathering); the progress rule and the bound on rounds decide whether another round runs. | The product loop, D6; owner 2026-09-04 (Fork C) | Decided |
| PL-16 | The update loop is fixed code: the update decision reads the worker's communication intent, a central situation's rules, and a response promise falling due. | The product loop, E1; decided 2026-10-09 | Decided |
| PL-17 | Memory and knowledge's package comes before each stage that reasons (understanding the arrival; the wider look; understanding the situation; gathering; deciding; writing), named by that stage; stages that are fixed code are observed, not served; a passed stage's package carries to the next stage. Replaces the codes R1–R3, W0–W3 and C1. | The product loop, F; owner 2026-10-28 ("Memory AI should run at every part of the loop"; "it would just be a wasteful run") | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-28; placed by stage 2026-10-29) |
| PL-18 | Each stage uses one kind of AI interaction from the harness study: fixed code; one structured answer; choosing among what was shown; plan, do and check, with explore steps; watching from outside. The kind is fixed per stage by the design. | The product loop, B; harness study, Part 1; owner 2026-10-29 ("discover what all kind of LLM loops are there") | Decided (the project owner, 2026-10-29) |
| PL-19 | A typed arrival with an exact reference is placed by code where it points and skips understanding and routing; one whose reference points to a completed situation goes through the arrival loop. | The product loop, C1 | Decided |
