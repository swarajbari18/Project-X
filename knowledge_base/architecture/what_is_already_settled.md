## Understanding comes first (settled 2026-10-29)

Tend understands each arrival before it acts on it, routing included.

The conversation manager writes a reading of each arrival: what is asked, what it refers to, time words turned into dates, the sender's claims, what is unknown, whether it is work at all.

Before the reading, memory and knowledge hands it what the arrival's words mean in this business.

Routing's candidates come from the reading, by exact values first. When the reading says a message refers to a past purchase, routing gets a small set of the sender's own closed situations (agreed 3 October), narrowed by the reading's values before any description is matched.

The worker understands new content before it plans or decides.

Using knowledge is not showing records: the business's own knowledge may help any step understand; records shown stay inside each step's boundary; the send check decides what each person may be told.

The routing queue is per person, not per channel address.

## Memory and knowledge (settled 2026-10-28)

Always called memory and knowledge, never just memory.

It runs beside every loop, on its own thread.

Before each part of a loop, the loop waits for its package: knowledge, or an explicit empty answer.

It sees the whole business, and decides nothing.

It creates and maintains the links between situations.

It keeps skills and procedures in object storage, versioned.

A business may write skills and connect its own MCP servers.

An outreach has one central situation, not a board.

Nothing is erased, except through the erasure tool's confirmation.

The design is the Level 2 page "how memory and knowledge works with the loops".

## The job

A delivery changes, and nobody is sitting on it.

Tend notices the change.

Tend chooses the next safe behaviour.

Tend makes the waiting visible.

Tend keeps the situation moving until someone safe takes responsibility.

The business decides the goal, the policy, and the information.

Tend acts inside that granted range.

When Tend must wait, it records the wait.

## When a message arrives

Meera writes "any update" from her own phone.

The phone is bound to Meera.

The words "this is Priya" do not make her Priya.

A human, including the owner, is verified by an identity provider before Tend treats them as that person.

A permission belongs to a verified person. A name or a phone number alone grants no permission.

Tend puts Meera's open situations in front of the choice, as short summaries.

If she also types a real order number, the situations are the open situations on that order.

A stranger who types the same order number does not receive Meera's situations.

The number is noticed and held back.

An investor who types Meera's order number does not receive Meera's situation.

The choice is attach, ask which one, start a new situation, or hold.

The full situation record opens only after one situation is chosen.

If the choice is ask or hold, and nothing is attached, no full situation record has to open.

The refund amount that needs a person is not one of those situations.

It is a setting.

It is read when a refund is attempted.

The situation rules above are what the architecture work uses.

The exact fields of that projection are deferred to Level 3 (field names of packages).

## What learning may change

Tend may keep a note that helps the next time.

A note must not change a business rule.

A note must not change a formal policy.

A note must not change a legal or compliance constraint.

A note must not change who may act.

A note must not change a permission.

A note must not change who owns a fact.

A note must not change a required approval.

A note must not change who is answerable.

A note must not move the line around an irreversible action.

The walked situations are examples of that ban.

One example is: a note must not rewrite a tracking status.

Another is: a note must not decide that a carrier outage means the business has no way to ship.

Those examples are not a shorter ban.

## Permission

Fetching a file runs its own check.

If the check allows it, the file comes back.

If the check refuses it, the worker receives that refusal.

The worker may then ask a person a question, when the answer changes the next safe step.

A refund reads the saved amount when the refund is attempted.

If that grant says a person must approve, the refund stops for that person.

The AI does not open a side conversation to approve itself.

Inside a grant, Tend may act.

A person is asked for approval only when that grant says so.

A grant is not a permanent "always allow."

## When a business document arrives from outside

A supplier's bill of materials, an invoice, or a balance sheet request can arrive without being asked for.

The intake step matches it to the situation for that sender, or to the sender's record, and files it there.

If nothing matches, a person sees it. No action is taken on the document alone.

## When a closed situation comes back

A closed situation stays closed.

It is reopened only when its closure was wrong: the same operational problem was resolved too early, or it has come back. The original is reopened, and the reason is recorded.

Any other new problem is a new situation, linked to the closed one.

A closed situation is looked at only when a message refers to it.
