# Findings — Meeting scheduling + Feedback timing (verified)

## 1. Scheduling/booking pain (SMB service businesses) — validated
- A service SMB spends **5–8 hours/week** on booking work (calls, WhatsApp replies, manual calendar
  entry, confirming, chasing no-shows). (Jogi AI)
- Booking via chat is "manual, messy, error-prone — forget to reply, double-book, lose track of who
  confirmed." The fix people sell: turn WhatsApp into a booking CHANNEL that links to a booking page,
  not a separate system. (Addagio)
- **40%+ of bookings happen outside business hours** — evenings/weekends/lunch. If you require a phone
  call during working hours you're invisible to ~half of clients ready to commit. (Jogi AI)
- No-shows ~20-30%; **reminders (24h + 2h before) cut no-shows 40-60%**. (Jogi AI, Pilot)
- **Real calendar = source of truth**; AI agent + booking link read Google/Outlook live → zero
  double-booking; automatic slot release on cancel; multi-touch reminders with Confirm/Reschedule/
  Cancel one-tap. (Pilot)
- WhatsApp **98% open rate**, 4/5 read within 5 min; customers want a response in ~10 min;
  reminders via WhatsApp >> email for opens. (Kommunicate)
- Booking patterns: "how to book in chat" + service menu + hours → WhatsApp auto-reply with booking
  link ("I'm with a client; book here; I confirm within 2 hours.")

## Why this matters for Tend's Meeting journey
The user's instinct — "calendar is free ≠ actually available; need employee preferences + meeting
type + business hours" — is **exactly** what production scheduling tools do via "rules that protect
your real calendar (minimum notice, capacity, personal blocks, vacations)" (Pilot). So Tend's Meeting
journey = an **availability + policy + preference engine** on top of calendars, not a plain "create
invite." Confirms the Gap.

## 2. Feedback timing (verified) — supports the "2-week default" hypothesis
- **Product satisfaction/feedback**: 1–4 weeks after delivery (Responsly); 14 days post-delivery deep
  product feedback is a standard sweet spot (Yotpo); CPG/beauty 7–14 days; apparel/home 30 days;
  high-end/subscription 45–60 days (KnoCommerce).
- NPS = relationship metric → 2–4 weeks (not transactional).
- Delivery-specific feedback = 1–2 days after arrival (judge packaging/speed).
- Don't survey too soon (noise) or too late (memory fade). Cap frequency to avoid fatigue.
- Close the loop: route low scores to support and ACT + tell the customer you acted ("you asked, we
  changed") — converts detractors into repeat buyers. (Responsly)
- Embedded first question in email improves completion (Responsly).

## Verdict
- User's "default 2 weeks" is validated as the general-purpose default. It must be a per-product
  business rule (consumable/digital → 7–14d; apparel/home → 30d; premium/subscription → 45–60d).
- Feedback must be **closed-loop**: low score → auto-route to support + acknowledge + act + follow
  up. That couples Feedback journey with Escalation + Owner journey.
- WhatsApp is high-open for notifications but initiating == template/opt-in compliance; email remains
  the general proactive default per user's instinct.
