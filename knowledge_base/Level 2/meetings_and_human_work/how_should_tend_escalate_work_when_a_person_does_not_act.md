# How should Tend escalate work when a person does not act?

## Where this question came from

Level 1 asks how Tend escalates work when a person does not act. This is close to the heart of the whole product — Product Vision names it the hardest problem: when the right next step needs a person to act and the person does not act, make the inaction visible and keep the situation moving until someone safe takes responsibility.

Human Collaboration already built the escalation model: distinguish not-delivered from not-acknowledged from no-progress, never treat silence as completion, and let escalation change urgency, visibility, notification, ownership or routing. This question ties that to the meeting world and adds the meeting-specific guard.

## Reuse, then add the meeting guard

The general escalation answer stands. On top of it, meetings add their own excuse for inaction: a person who repeatedly reserves a meeting and never keeps it is spending other people's time, and that specific pattern is guarded by the two counters (see the cancelled/missed/rescheduled question).

## The person-level floor: the escalation is terminal

When the person counter crosses its floor, the meeting chain escalates **once, to a senior, and then it ends**:

- The prospect or customer can no longer book meetings on their own. When they ask, they get the same message: the limit is exhausted.
- The senior person receives the full report: the complete history of this person across their situations, the repeated misses or reschedules, why Tend set routine booking aside.
- There is no third rung and no new booking ladder. The senior alone decides whether they want to reach out to the employee through Tend. That is the terminal decision.

This terminal is deliberate. An endless cascade past the senior would be redundant and would pester the very people whose attention we are protecting.

## The terminal still must not be silent on the senior

We did not let "no further escalation" become "the senior's own inaction is invisible." Product Vision refuses silent inaction. So:

- **No meeting escalation ladder past the senior** — this stays. The meeting-complaint chain genuinely ends at the senior.
- **The senior's own inaction is surfaced** — if the senior receives the report and does nothing, that is now business inaction at the senior level. It is not given a new meeting ladder; it goes through the existing machinery — the owner-attention filter and Failure. The owner is the last accountability authority.

So the senior is the last meeting authority; the owner is the last authority over accountability. "No more escalation" refers to the meeting chain, not to the product's refusal to be silent anywhere.

## What this question does not settle

- The exact floor number and escalation timers (business configuration / later research, with a research-informed default).
- Who the senior or owner is in a given business (configuration, Authority and Ownership).

## Working decision

When a person does not act, reuse the escalation model, and for the meeting's specific waste-guard use the two counters. Past the person-level floor, the meeting chain escalates once to a senior with the full report and then is terminal for meetings; but the senior's own inaction is still surfaced through the owner / Failure machinery, so nothing is silently dead.
