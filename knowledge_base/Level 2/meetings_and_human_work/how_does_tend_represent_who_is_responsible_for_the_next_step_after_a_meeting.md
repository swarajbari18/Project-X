# Who is responsible for the next step after a meeting?

## Where this question came from

Level 1 asks how Tend represents who is responsible for the next step after a meeting. Human Collaboration already answers the general version: one responsible owner per human work item, work can be transferred, and handoff history stays visible. This question is the general answer applied to the moment right after a meeting.

## Our current answer

A meeting does not end as a blank. Its outcome creates the next step, and that next step is human work with one responsible owner — the same rule as any human work item.

Product Vision names the two main outcomes: the prospect decides to buy, or the prospect wants time to think. And it adds: Tend captures what was discussed and works out what to do next in either case.

So after the meeting:

- **If they bought** — the next step is whatever comes next (order, onboarding, next actions), owned by the person who must carry it.
- **If they want time** — the next step is a "give them time, do not pester, but do not forget" wait, owned so that a gentle follow-up exists at the right cadence.
- **Any follow-ups or new questions from the call** — each becomes its own next step or new situation, each with a responsible owner.

## How the owner is chosen

The next step uses Human Collaboration's "correct responsibility target": role, authority, availability, business rules. It may be the person who ran the meeting, or a different role entirely (the salesperson who calls, the person who processes the order, the person who owns the follow-up). One accountable owner per work item; the rest are contributors or informed participants.

## Who does not own it

Tend does not quietly absorb the follow-up. Tend records the outcome, decides the next behaviour (communicate a next step, wait at the right cadence, create the work), and routes the work to a person. The business owns the decision of what actually happens next; Tend coordinates who must act and keeps it visible.

## What this question does not settle

- The exact outcome taxonomy beyond bought / wants-time / follow-ups (business configuration / later research).
- Channel details of the follow-up (Channels & Permissions, Level 3).

## Working decision

The next step after a meeting is human work with one responsible owner, chosen by the same rules as any human work item. The outcome — bought, wants time, or follow-ups — decides what that next step is; Tend coordinates the who and keeps it moving, and the business owns what actually happens next.
