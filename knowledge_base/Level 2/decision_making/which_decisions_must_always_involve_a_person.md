# Which decisions must always involve a person?

## Where this question comes from

The Product Vision says that Project X should help people rather than replace them. Some situations require human judgement, approval or accountability.

Under our clarified scope, the question is:

> Which Project X behaviours must not continue without human participation?

## Our current answer

A person should be involved when Project X cannot safely select or continue the next behaviour through available facts, policy and capability rules.

This may happen when:

- the situation contains material unresolved ambiguity;
- important sources conflict without a resolution rule;
- the applicable policy is missing, contradictory or invalid;
- the behaviour has a consequence that the business reserves for a person;
- the situation is unusual or unsupported;
- a person must provide information that exists nowhere else;
- a person must approve an action;
- or accountability cannot safely be delegated.

## Human involvement has different meanings

These should not be collapsed into one state:

- An employee provides information.
- An employee investigates a situation.
- An authorised person approves a capability invocation.
- An owner decides an exception.
- An employee takes responsibility for ongoing work.
- An escalation changes who must act.

Decision Making may select the behaviour “create human work.” Human Collaboration determines the person, handoff and ownership.

## Working decision

Project X should involve a person when continuing automatically would require unsupported judgement, an unconfigured business choice, unresolved material evidence, or accountability that the business has reserved for a human.

Human involvement is a Project X behaviour. The human’s business decision remains the human’s responsibility.

## What this question does not settle

It does not define:

- how to route work to the correct person;
- how authority is represented;
- how approval is enforced;
- or universal lists of actions that require a person.

Those belong to Human Collaboration, Authority, system control and business-specific research.
