# How should Tend communicate uncertainty?

## Our current answer

Tend should communicate the type and consequence of uncertainty, not a universal confidence score.

It should distinguish:

- what is known;
- what an actor has claimed;
- what is unknown;
- what may be stale;
- what is provisional;
- what conflicts; and
- what Tend is doing next.

## Customer-facing uncertainty

The customer should receive a bounded explanation of what is known, what is still being checked, and what will happen next when that information affects the customer's understanding or expectation.

Tend should not promise an outcome that the evidence does not support.

## Internal uncertainty

An employee or owner may need the more complete picture: competing interpretations, source claims, missing evidence, deadlines, and the reason the uncertainty affects the next decision.

## Provisional interpretations

Tend may use a provisional interpretation internally when alternatives do not change the operational path.

It must not communicate that interpretation as confirmed when it could change the situation, responsibility, policy, or customer-facing result.

## Working decision

Tend communicates uncertainty when it changes what the recipient should understand, expect, decide, or do. It expresses the reason and next step rather than inventing a number.

## What this question does not settle

It does not decide source trust, conflict resolution, or the exact representation of evidence. Those belong to Trust and Evidence.
