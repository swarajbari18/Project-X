# Which actor owns each type of information?

## The short answer

No single actor "owns" an information type in the absolute sense. Ownership is claim-specific, and it is separate from source, truth and permission.

## Our answer

Ownership of information belongs to the actor responsible for the underlying information or operation across its lifecycle — maintaining it, updating it, confirming it and correcting it when necessary. This is the working definition we already settled in the Gathering Information category, and it carries over here unchanged.

Examples:

- A logistics provider owns its delivery operation and tracking record.
- The business owns the customer relationship, its own records and its promises.
- An external system owns the record it maintains and is responsible for its accuracy.
- A customer is authoritative about their own experience, but does not own the carrier's operational record.
- Tend owns the coordination of the situation and its own record of what it gathered, understood and communicated.

## Ownership is not the same as three other things

We spent real effort separating these, and Authority and Ownership is where they must stay separate:

- **Owner** = responsible for the record / operation.
- **Source** = where a particular claim came from.
- **Authority** = claim-specific responsibility (who the business trusts for that type).
- **Permission** = who may see or disclose it.

An actor can own information without being allowed to disclose it to everyone. Tend may use a confidential source internally while sending only a limited description externally.

## What this resolves

Because authority is claim- and owner-specific, Tend never treats one source as universally authoritative. "Owns this type of information" is a shorthand for "is responsible for this claim type," not a universal ranking.

## Carry-over to the rest of the category

This split is what lets "which actor owns each decision" and "which actor owns each action" be answered properly — those are separate questions, handled in their own documents. Ownership of information, of a decision and of an action are not the same thing.
