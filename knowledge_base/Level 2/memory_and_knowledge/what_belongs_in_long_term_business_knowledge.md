# What belongs in long-term business knowledge?

## Status

Answered — working decision, pending review.

## The distinction

Long-term business knowledge is reusable information that can help Tend understand or coordinate future situations for the same business.

It is not simply a larger conversation history.

It is a derived and scoped view of experience, business material and observations.

## Possible kinds of business knowledge

### Local vocabulary

What employees mean when they use internal terms, abbreviations, nicknames or process language.

### Entity relationships

How the business refers to teams, locations, products, customers, partners, tools and internal concepts.

### Operational conventions

How work is normally performed in practice.

### Recurring exception patterns

Situations that repeatedly look normal but require a different next step.

### Tool and source guidance

Which source or capability normally contains a particular kind of information, and what preconditions Tend repeatedly needs to check.

### Procedural lessons

Reusable guidance about how Tend can perform a recurring task more reliably.

## Descriptive is not normative

An observed workaround may be useful knowledge about how people currently work.

It is not automatically an official business procedure.

An employee may say:

> We usually bypass this step when the warehouse is busy.

Tend may remember that this happens.

It must not conclude that the bypass is permitted unless the business’s authority and policy responsibilities establish that.

## Scope is part of the knowledge

Knowledge must carry the business, process, role, capability, time and condition in which it applies.

The statement “ready means packed” is incomplete.

The useful statement is closer to:

> In Business A’s warehouse process, employees use “ready” to mean packed and awaiting carrier collection.

## Working decision

Long-term business knowledge contains reusable, scoped and traceable meanings, relationships, conventions, exceptions and procedural guidance.

It remains distinguishable from formal policy, source-of-truth records, current situation state and raw conversation history.
