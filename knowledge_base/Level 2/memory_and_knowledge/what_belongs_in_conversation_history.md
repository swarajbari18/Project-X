# What belongs in conversation history?

## Status

Answered — working decision, pending review.

## Conversation history is a record of communication

Conversation history answers:

> What was communicated, by whom, when, through which channel, and in what direction?

It is not the same as long-term business knowledge.

The history should preserve enough context to reconstruct the interaction and understand later corrections.

That includes:

- incoming and outgoing messages;
- participants and roles;
- channel and direction;
- timestamps;
- message references;
- attachments or referenced artifacts;
- the situation to which a message was attached;
- interpretation and routing results; and
- later corrections to those interpretations.

## Tend does not become the owner of the message

The canonical message remains with the communication system that owns it.

Tend keeps the references and the working representation needed to coordinate the situation.

This follows the earlier decision that Tend coordinates communication but does not become the source of truth for messages or business records.

## A message can be history without becoming knowledge

The message may be:

- directly relevant to a situation;
- indirectly referenced by a situation;
- retained for audit;
- excluded from the current context; or
- used as evidence for a future learning item.

None of these automatically makes the message a reusable business fact.

## Corrections do not rewrite history

If Tend misunderstood a message, the original message remains unchanged.

The interpretation is corrected in a later situation-model version or learning record.

The record should show:

- what was originally said;
- what Tend understood;
- what was later corrected; and
- what future learning was created, if any.

## Working decision

Conversation history is an immutable communication and evidence record connected to situation versions and experience records.

It is not automatically long-term business knowledge and is not automatically injected into future context.
