# The Situation Registrar: plans

**What it is.** The Situation Registrar keeps each business problem's history, sends word to the right parts of Tend, keeps the list of every situation, and writes what other parts decide. It decides nothing itself. Think of a court's registrar: it keeps the record, checks that a filing is in the right form but never whether it is right, and serves notices, while the judge decides. The design's older files call it the situation record.
