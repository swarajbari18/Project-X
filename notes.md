On batching vs category-by-category
My recommendation: group, but only the ones that genuinely share one spine, and never more than two at a time.
Here's the reasoning. The completed categories weren't hard because they had many questions — they were hard because each one had to find a single idea that the whole category turned on (Authority's idea was "Tend only acts inside a granted range"; Decision Making's was "Tend picks its own next behaviour, not the business outcome"). That single idea is where the real cognitive work happens, and it can't be hurried.
But two of the remaining categories share that idea between them:
- Coordination = how independent parts work together, and how long-running work stays alive, visible, not-duplicated, and recoverable.
- Time = how the system reacts when situations change just because time passed — deadlines, scheduled starts, waiting that ends, forgotten work.
Both are really one spine: "how the system lives through time and across actors." The word "waiting" appears in both lists. I'd do them as one continuous thread. Everything else — Failure, Explainability/Observation, Journey/Lifecycle, Meetings, Channels, Growth, Architecture, Compliance — I'd keep as separate sessions, because each needs its own spine and they don't share one.
Concretely, my proposed order for the remaining work:
1. Coordination + Time (one batch — this is what we start now)
2. Failure
3. Explainability + Observation (these two share a spine too)
4. Journey/Lifecycle + Business View (owner's view of the journey — related)
5. Meetings/Human Work
6. Channels/Permissions
7. Growth/Evolution (feeds Architecture)
8. Compliance/Security
9. Architecture — last, as you said
That collapses 11 sessions into ~7 while keeping each batch mentally coherent.
