We just completed **Meetings and Human Work** — the Level 2 category for choosing who meets and keeping a reserved human's time honest.

What we decided (the short version):

- A meeting is a **wait on the shared Coordination/Time spine**: a decision → a wait whose subject is one or more people and a date → on no-show/cancel/reschedule the wait fails as a **meeting event** → the outcome becomes human work. We do not build a parallel calendar model; we consume the spine via `the_meeting_as_a_wait_spine_bridge.md`.
- A real slot = **eligibility ∩ calendared availability ∩ each participant's expressed intent.** "Free on the calendar is not genuinely available." Calendared availability can only say a slot could exist; only the person's intent says they will take it, so we never guess it.
- Intent is **gathered once and booked inside** (not a per-slot yes/no), so multi-person joins scale. Employee standing intent is a stored, expiring preference (default monthly) with a chosen mode: **absolute-and-inform** for routine, **check-first** for decision points; the meeting type sets the default and business rules/employee preferences override.
- Two counter floors, both **derived** from recorded meeting events (never stored as a new person-master record): a **story counter** that quietly demands fresh intent before re-booking in that story, and a **person counter** that, past its floor, escalates to a senior with the full history. The floor is always a **routing change, never a silent rejection**; the meeting escalation is terminal past the senior, but the senior's own inaction still surfaces through the owner/Failure machinery.
- Reuse: Human Collaboration owns the responsibility target and the escalation model; the external-partner contact is one escalation effect on the same availability-and-intent shape; Business View owns what the owner sees.

The full depth lives in `knowledge_base/Level 2/meetings_and_human_work/`, and the research is in `research/findings_scheduling_feedback.md` plus a new research record on scheduling/availability/intent.
